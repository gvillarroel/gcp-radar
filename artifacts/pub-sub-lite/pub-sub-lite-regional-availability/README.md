## Technical Summary

Pub/Sub Lite is available in additional regions including Hong Kong, Tokyo, Osaka, Seoul, Mumbai, Jakarta, Warsaw, Montreal, Sao Paulo, Northern Virginia, Salt Lake City, and Las Vegas.
