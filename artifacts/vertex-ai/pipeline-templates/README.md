## Technical Summary

Pipeline Templates are now generally available, with the Your Templates tab supported by Artifact Registry for publishing and curating pipeline and component templates; Vertex AI introduced Pipeline Templates in Preview to create, publish, and manage reusable machine-learning pipeline templates, including running and filtering pipeline executions.
