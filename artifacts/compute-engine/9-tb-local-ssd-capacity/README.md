# 9 TB Local SSD capacity

Product: Compute Engine
Feature slug: `9-tb-local-ssd-capacity`
Coverage: `MEDIUM`
IAM mapping: `unknown`

## Technical Summary

9 TB Local SSD capacity lets N1, N2, and N2D instances use up to 24 local SSD partitions totaling 9 TB; 9 TB Local SSD capacity allows Compute Engine instances to use up to 24 local SSD partitions for 9 TB of local SSD space.
