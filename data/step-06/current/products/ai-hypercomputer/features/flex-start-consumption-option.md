# Flex-start consumption option

Product: AI Hypercomputer
Coverage: HIGH

## Step 02 Summary

Flex-start lets users obtain secured-pool GPU capacity in advance for up to seven days, improving chances of acquiring high-demand resources.

## Extended Definition

Flex-start is a consumption option in AI Hypercomputer that provisions capacity from a secured resource pool. The feature allows users to obtain resources up to seven days in advance, and release notes also mention GA support for compact placement policies for standalone Flex-start VMs.

## Evidence Summary
