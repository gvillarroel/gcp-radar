Google Cloud's terms of service define the
deprecation policy that applies to Apigee. The
deprecation policy only applies to the features
or services listed therein.
After an Apigee feature has been officially deprecated, the
feature continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in
the terms of service . After this period of time, the
feature or service is scheduled for shutdown.
