Export recommendations to BigQuery
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
To view daily snapshots of all recommendations for your organization, including
role recommendations, you can export your recommendations to BigQuery.
To export your recommendations to BigQuery, you need to set up a
data transfer using BigQuery Data Transfer Service. To learn how to set up a data
transfer, see Export recommendations to BigQuery .
