The deprecation policy that applies to Gemini for
Google Cloud is defined in the Google Cloud
terms of service (section 1) as
"Discontinuation of Services." The deprecation policy only applies to the
features and services .
After Google deprecates a Gemini for Google Cloud feature,
the feature is available for at least the period of time defined in the terms of
service. After this period of time, Google schedules the feature or service for
shutdown.
