Browse agents with Agent Gallery
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
Agent Gallery is your central hub for discovering, using, and managing agents. To access the gallery, go to your Gemini Enterprise app and click Agents in the app navigation menu. The gallery displays all available agents, organized into three categories:
Made by Google: ready-to-use agents that serve a specific function, such
as
Deep Research .
From your organization: agents that your organization has created or
shared with you.
Your agents: custom agents that you can create using
Agent Designer .
Note: In the Gemini Enterprise Frontline edition, access to
agents added by your organization is limited to agents that have been
provisioned for you by your administrator. Full access to your organization's
agents requires the Gemini Enterprise Standard or Gemini Enterprise
Plus editions.
Made by Google agents aren't available in the Gemini Enterprise
Frontline edition.
You can use Agent Gallery to do the following:
Select and use any available agent to start a conversation and get help with your tasks.
Create a new custom agent using Agent Designer.
Edit or delete the custom agents you have created.
