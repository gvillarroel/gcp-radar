You discover, select, and deploy software packages from the
Cloud Marketplace page of
the Google Cloud console.
Before you begin
Verify that you have the required Identity and Access Management (IAM) roles to
purchase or deploy products. For details, see IAM roles for
purchasing and managing
products .
Verify that you're in the Google Cloud project in which you want to
purchase or deploy the product.
Search for a product
You can discover software packages by searching Cloud Marketplace.
In addition to standard keyword search, the search experience includes
AI-powered features to help you find the best products for your use case through
conversational queries and problem descriptions. For more information about
using these features, see Discover products with AI
search .
The Cloud Marketplace search experience includes the following features:
Instant Search : As you type in the search bar, a drop-down menu
instantly shows query suggestions, category matches, and specific product or
partner matches. You can select an instant match to navigate directly to a
product or category.
AI Overviews : When you run a search, an AI Overview summarizes how the
marketplace inventory addresses your query. This includes a table of the
most relevant products, the providers, and a justification for why each
product matches your intent.
AI Mode : For complex queries, you can use the conversational AI
Mode . Enter AI Mode by clicking the AI button in the search bar, or by
clicking Dive Deep with AI Mode or a contextual chip from an AI
Overview. AI Mode lets you have a multi-turn conversation to refine your
product discovery.
Filters : You can refine your search results using the horizontal filters
on the search page, such as Category , Type , Price , and
Partner . Both the standard search results and AI Overviews respect your
active filters.
Deploy a software package
To deploy a software package from Google Cloud Marketplace, complete the following
steps:
In Cloud Marketplace, search for the product that you want to deploy.
For products that have ratings and reviews, the search results page displays
the average rating and review count on each product card to help you
evaluate solutions.
Visit the listing page for the product that you want to deploy. From the
Product details page, you can review details of the product, its pricing
information, and if available, its user ratings and reviews.
From the Product details page, read the relevant terms and agreements
for the product. These terms and agreements represent the current version.
When you purchase or deploy the product, you must click to accept them as
part of the purchase or deployment process.
You see one of the following buttons, depending on the type of product that
you want to deploy:
For software as a service (SaaS) products, you see a button that says
Subscribe , or a button that says View all plans .
For virtual machine (VM) products, you see a button that says
Launch , or a button that says Get .
For Kubernetes products, you see a button that says Purchase , a
button that says Configure , or a button that says Get .
For AI models, you see a button that says Enable . For more
information, see Get AI models from
Google Cloud Marketplace .
To deploy the software package, click the button that you see.
For some products, you might be asked to complete a form that appears by
providing details of your intended usage. If you encounter a form, complete
the required fields and click Next .
Read the relevant terms and agreements for the product that appear as part
of the purchasing or deployment flow.
Note: The terms and agreements that you see when you purchase or deploy a
product represent the current versions. If you previously viewed the terms
and agreements from the product's Product details page, verify the
current terms and agreements when you accept them during purchasing or
deployment.
To accept the relevant terms and agreements and complete your purchase or
deployment of the product, click Agree .
