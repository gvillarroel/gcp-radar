Explore AI agents on Cloud Marketplace

Google Cloud Marketplace includes a dedicated page for AI agents, allowing you to
discover, evaluate, and deploy conversational agents tailored to your business
needs.
This page explains how to leverage the new AI search specifically for finding AI
agents, how to browse the available listings, and how to use the available
filters.
The dedicated AI agents page
The AI agents page on
Cloud Marketplace
provides a curated view of the AI agents available for deployment. It highlights
agents that can automate tasks, answer questions, or provide specialized domain
knowledge.
Leverage AI search for finding AI agents
You can use the conversational AI search experience to find AI agents that solve
specific business problems. For more details on using the AI search experience,
see
Discover products with AI search .
To find an AI agent using AI search:
In the Cloud Marketplace search bar, click the AI button to enter
AI Mode .
Describe your business problem or the task you want an AI agent to perform.
For example, "I need an AI agent that can handle customer support inquiries
for an ecommerce platform," or "Find an AI agent to help analyze
financial data."
The AI assistant will provide an overview of relevant AI agents, explaining
how each agent matches your requirements.
Browse and evaluate AI agent listings
When you search for AI agents or browse the dedicated page, you can evaluate the
available options using the provided product cards and AI-generated summaries.
Review the AI Overview or the AI Mode conversation for a summary of how
the suggested AI agents address your query.
Examine the product cards for each AI agent, which display key metadata,
such as the provider and a brief description.
Click the link to an AI agent's details page to review its features,
pricing, and deployment options.
Use filters for AI agents
To help you refine your search, you can use the horizontal filters on the search
page. When you apply filters, both the standard search results and the AI
Overviews respect your active filters.
For AI agents, you can use the following new and updated filters to narrow down
the available options:
Category : Filter AI agents by their general classification or domain.
Industry : Filter AI agents that are designed or specialized for specific
industries, such as healthcare, finance, or retail.
Business function : Filter AI agents based on the organizational function
they serve, such as customer support, sales, marketing, or human resources.
Compatibility : Filter AI agents based on their compatibility, for example
A2A and Gemini Enterprise.
By combining the conversational AI search with these filters, you can quickly
find the most relevant AI agents for your specific use cases.
