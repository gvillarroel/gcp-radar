You can get AI models from Google Cloud Marketplace and use them with
Model Garden.
Before you begin
Verify that you have the required Identity and Access Management (IAM) roles to
purchase or deploy products. For details, see
IAM roles for purchasing and managing products .
Verify that you're in the Google Cloud project in which you want to purchase
or deploy the product.
Get AI models from Cloud Marketplace
To get AI models from Cloud Marketplace, complete the following steps:
In Cloud Marketplace, visit the product page for the AI model that you
want to get.
From the product page, click Enable .
For some AI models, you might be asked to complete a form that appears by
providing details of your intended usage. If you encounter a form, complete
the required fields and click Next .
Review the relevant terms and agreements for the AI model that appear as part
of the purchasing or deployment flow.
Note: The terms and agreements that you see when you purchase or deploy a
product represent the current versions at that time. If you previously viewed
the terms and agreements from an AI model's Product details page, we
recommend that you verify the current terms and agreements when you accept
them during purchase or deployment.
To accept the relevant terms and agreements and finish enabling the AI model,
click Agree .
Regionalized pricing for AI models
Some AI models on Cloud Marketplace have pricing that changes depending on
where you use the AI model. In these cases, Cloud Marketplace determines
the pricing based on the location of the endpoint to which you're sending your
requests.
If an AI model's pricing varies based on location, you can see the pricing by
location in the Pricing section of the AI model's Cloud Marketplace
listing. To view pricing for different locations, use the Location
drop-down.
If you're using Google Cloud across multiple regions, verify that you're
sending your requests to an endpoint in your preferred location.
Use AI models with Vertex AI
After you've enabled an AI model, complete the following steps to use it in
Model Garden:
In Cloud Marketplace, visit the product page for the AI model that you
want to use.
From the AI model's product page, click Manage on Vertex AI .
For more information about using Model Garden, visit the
Model Garden documentation .
What's next
For information about managing your usage of AI models, see
Manage AI models .
