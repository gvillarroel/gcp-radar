Organization governance for Google Cloud Marketplace
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
Google Cloud Marketplace gives you ways to manage your organization's procurement of
Cloud Marketplace products. This supports you as you limit your
organization's risk from unauthorized software purchases and deployment.
Changes that you make to governance settings affect your entire organization ,
the root node of the Google hierarchy of resources.
Google Cloud Private Marketplace
Google Cloud Private Marketplace lets
organization administrators curate collections of approved and procured products
to share with their entire organization, an organizational unit (folder), or a
specific project. After you've turned on Google Cloud Private Marketplace, your users access your
private marketplace by default, though they can also access the public
Google Cloud Marketplace.
Procurement requests
You can use procurement requests to let your users request that you assess and
authorize products for use within your organization. For more information, see
Manage procurement requests .
