Add products to a collection
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
After you've created a collection, you can add additional products to it.
Note: Make sure that Google Cloud Private Marketplace supports the products that you want to add to
your collection. For details, see
Supported products .
Add products
To add products to a collection that you've already created, complete the
following steps:
Ensure that you have the required IAM roles .
In Cloud Marketplace, click Marketplace Governance .
Go to Marketplace
On the Private Marketplace page, select the collection that you want to
add products to.
In Add products , click + Add .
In the field that you see, enter the URL of the product's product details
page, and then click Add . Cloud Marketplace retrieves the product's
information.
To add the product to the collection, click Save .
Continue adding products until your collection is complete.
Click Save to save your changes to your collection.
