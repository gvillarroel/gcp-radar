Google Cloud Marketplace includes an AI-powered search experience to help you discover
software packages, services, and AI agents for your specific business needs.
This document introduces the conversational AI search experience, how to
interact with the AI assistant, and how to understand and refine your search
results.
The conversational AI search experience
Unlike conventional keyword search, the AI-powered search experience lets you
describe your goals, use cases, or technical problems using natural language.
The system then identifies relevant products and provides a summarized
explanation of how they meet your requirements.
You can access these features from the search bar on the Cloud Marketplace
page
in the Google Cloud console.
Search features
The AI-powered search experience includes several key features:
Instant Search : As you type, the search bar instantly displays
autocomplete suggestions, relevant categories, and specific products or
partners. You can select an instant match to navigate directly to it.
AI Overviews : When you run a standard search, an AI Overview summarizes
how the marketplace inventory addresses your query. This includes a table of
relevant products and providers, with a justification for why each product
matches your intent.
AI Mode : For complex inquiries, you can engage in a
conversation in AI Mode . Enter AI Mode by clicking the AI button in
the search bar, or by clicking Dive Deep with AI Mode from an AI
Overview. This dedicated interface lets you ask follow-up questions and
refine your search iteratively.
Filters : You can refine your search results using the horizontal filters
on the search page, such as Category , Type , Price , and
Partner . Both the standard search results and AI Overviews respect your
active filters.
Note: The search function is only available for the AI Agent product
category.
Interact with the AI assistant
You can search by describing the problem you want to solve, the type of software
you need, or the industry you operate in.
Examples of effective queries
For effective results, structure your queries to provide context about your use
case or problem.
Use case-based : "I need a solution to help manage large-scale data
migrations to Google Cloud."
Problem descriptions : "What products can help me secure my Kubernetes
clusters against unauthorized access?"
Attribute-based : "Show me marketing analytics platforms that integrate
with BigQuery."
AI Agents : "Find an AI agent for customer support in the financial
industry."
Understand the search results
When you use AI Mode or view an AI Overview, the results include:
AI-generated summaries : An explanation of how the suggested products
address your specific query.
In-conversation links : Direct links to the suggested products' details
pages so you can view pricing and deployment options.
Refine your search
If the initial results don't exactly match your needs, you can refine your
discovery process:
Ask follow-up questions : In AI Mode, you can ask questions to narrow
down the results. For example, "Which of these are available as a SaaS
product?" or "Show me options from specific partners."
Apply horizontal filters : Use the filters on the search page, such as
Category , Type , Price , and Partner , to narrow down the
results. AI Overviews and the standard search results respect your active
filters.
