Turn Enterprise edition on or off for search apps
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
This page describes how to turn Enterprise edition
on or off for a search app.
Enterprise Edition is only available for search apps; it does not apply to
recommendations or chat agents.
You can't turn off Enterprise edition for apps that are connected to a website
data store.
Before you begin
If you're turning on Enterprise edition features, be aware of
additional costs that this can incur. See Pricing .
Make sure that any app for which you turn off Enterprise edition doesn't require
Enterprise edition features. After turning Enterprise edition off, all features
that require Enterprise edition stop working in your app within a few minutes.
Features that require Enterprise edition include:
Website search (both Standard and Enterprise editions)
Extractive answers
Extractive segments
Core generative answers
Turn Enterprise edition on or off
To turn Enterprise edition on or off, follow these steps:
In the Google Cloud console, go to the AI Applications > Apps page.
AI Applications
Click the name of the app for which you want to turn Enterprise edition
features on or off.
In the navigation menu, click Configurations .
Click the Response Settings tab.
In the Enterprise edition features pane, click the toggle on or off.
This option is only available for search apps that support enterprise edition
features. This option can't be turned off for apps with website data stores.
Click Save . Your changes take place within a few minutes.
