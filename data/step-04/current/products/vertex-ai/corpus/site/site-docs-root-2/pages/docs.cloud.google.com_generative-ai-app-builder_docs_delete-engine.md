This page describes how to delete a Vertex AI Search app.
The following instructions are for the Google Cloud console.
Console
To delete an app, follow these steps:
In the Google Cloud console, go to the AI Applications page.
AI Applications
For the app that you want to delete, click
more_vert , and then click Delete .
In the Delete the app? dialog, enter your app name, and then
click Confirm .
