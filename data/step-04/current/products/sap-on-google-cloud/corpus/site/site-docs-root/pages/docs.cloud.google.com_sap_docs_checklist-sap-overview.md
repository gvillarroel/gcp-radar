Overview of the SAP on Google Cloud checklists
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
When implementing SAP on Google Cloud, we know that your goal is to bring
up your landscape easily and with as few issues as possible. To help you succeed
in this goal, we've gathered feedback from customers, our Professional Services
team, and support staff who have implemented instances of SAP on
Google Cloud. The following checklists provide you with
recommendations learned from these experts to help you be as successful as
possible when bringing up your own SAP on Google Cloud landscape.
The checklists offer helpful guidelines to follow when migrating your SAP
workloads to Google Cloud. They outline many aspects that you should
define in your architecture so that you're ready for the migration.
We know that your business requirements and processes are unique. You
know what works for you, but consider the common themes and guidelines
included in this set of checklists to ensure the success of your journey to
Google Cloud. The following checklists are available:
All SAP landscapes on Google Cloud
SAP HANA on Google Cloud
SAP on Google Cloud with other databases
SAP NetWeaver on Google Cloud
High availability for SAP on Google Cloud
Note:
If you have any questions or issues during your migration, please
contact your Google Cloud account team, your selected cloud services
partner, or open a support case by signing into the
Google Cloud console Support page .
These checklists contain general recommendations and might not cover all
aspects of your SAP on Google Cloud landscape.
