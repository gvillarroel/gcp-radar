Turn off Endpoint Verification

This document shows how administrators can turn off Endpoint Verification for their
organizational units.
After you turn off Endpoint Verification, if you add any devices to your
organization unit, you won't see those devices in the Google Admin console.
You will continue to see the devices that were monitored before, however,
the device information is no longer updated.
To turn off Endpoint Verification for your organizational unit, do the following:
In the Google Workspace Admin console, go to the Devices page.
Go to Devices
In the navigation menu, click Mobile & endpoints > Settings > Universal .
In the Universal settings pane, click Data access .
In the Data access pane, click Device signals .
Clear the Monitor which devices access organization data checkbox.
Click Save .
