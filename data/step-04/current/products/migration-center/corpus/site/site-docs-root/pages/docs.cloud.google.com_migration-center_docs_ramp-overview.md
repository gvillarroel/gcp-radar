Rapid Migration and Modernization Program (RaMP) overview
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
The Rapid Migration and Modernization Program (RaMP) is a Google Cloud
global program for migration and modernization initiatives. RaMP combines
Google Cloud products and best practices into a unified approach to help
you achieve business goals and mitigate common challenges. RaMP provides a
prescriptive framework, resources, and tools to help you execute migrations
and modernizations to Google Cloud.
To learn more about RaMP, see the following pages:
RaMP: your fast track to an AI-ready cloud
Fast-track your cloud migration with RaMP
How RaMP works
RaMP follows the following phased approach to guide your migration and
modernization journey.
Assess
We work with your team to perform a thorough, data-driven discovery of your
existing environment. Using our advanced tools, we help you identify the
highest-value opportunities and workloads, perform total cost of ownership
calculations, and establish performance benchmarks to start to maximize
immediate business value.
In this phase, use Google Cloud Migration Center to discover source infrastructure and
assessment to understand your existing environment.
