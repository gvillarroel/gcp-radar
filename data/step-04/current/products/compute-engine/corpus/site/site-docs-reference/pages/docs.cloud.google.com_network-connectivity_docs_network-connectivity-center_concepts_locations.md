Locations supported for data transfer
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
If you want to use site-to-site-data-transfer, you must create the
Network Connectivity Center (NCC) spokes in regions that support this feature.
Supported regions include all of those in the following countries:
Africa
South Africa
APAC
Australia, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Taiwan
Europe
Belgium, Finland, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Sweden,
Switzerland, United Kingdom
Middle East
Israel, Qatar
North America
Canada, Mexico, United States
South America
Brazil, Chile
Any restrictions on the use of telecommunications services in your customer
agreement are not applicable to the use of NCC's data transfer
functionality in these locations.
In Japan, NCC is provided through Google Connect Asia
Pacific Pte Ltd.
