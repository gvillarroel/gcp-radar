Admin settings - Alerts & Schedules pages
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
These pages appear under the Alerts & Schedules section of Looker's Admin menu:
Alerts :
View, filter by, edit, and delete dashboard tile alerts. Navigate to the dashboard containing the tile with the alert.
Alert History :
View running, complete, and failed alerts.
Schedules :
View, reassign, and delete data delivery schedules.
Schedule History :
View history of and troubleshoot scheduled data deliveries.
Scheduled Emails :
Manage email delivery settings to include only links back to Looker content, only Looker data, or both links and data. Monitor content being sent from Looker to email addresses with domains external to Looker.
