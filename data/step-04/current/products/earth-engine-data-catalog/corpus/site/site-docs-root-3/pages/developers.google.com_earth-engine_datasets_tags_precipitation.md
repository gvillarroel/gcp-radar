outlined_flag
Numerous datasets provide climate and precipitation information at various spatial and temporal resolutions globally and for specific regions like the US.
Datasets cover reanalysis, forecasts, historical observations, and downscaled climate projections.
Precipitation and temperature are common variables, along with other climate parameters like humidity, evapotranspiration, and drought indices.
Several international and national organizations like ECMWF, NOAA, NASA, JAXA, and academic institutions are sources for this data.
