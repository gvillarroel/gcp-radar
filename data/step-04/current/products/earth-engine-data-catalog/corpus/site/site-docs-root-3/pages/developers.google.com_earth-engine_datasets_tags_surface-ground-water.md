outlined_flag
The webpage describes several datasets related to water bodies, hydrology, and water-related risks, including global lakes bathymetry, surface water maps, and drought and flood indices.
Datasets cover various aspects such as water area identification, monthly water mass anomalies, watershed boundaries, and global river flood hazard.
The HydroATLAS project provides hydro-environmental attributes for global watersheds at different detail levels, while HydroSHEDS offers consistent global hydrographic information including river networks and drainage data.
These datasets utilize various sources like satellite imagery (Landsat, MODIS), elevation data (SRTM), and hydrological modeling for comprehensive water-related information globally or regionally.
