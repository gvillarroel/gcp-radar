outlined_flag
This page contains datasets related to plant productivity, including measures like NDVI, albedo, vegetation height, Gross Primary Productivity (GPP), Net Primary Production (NPP), Leaf Area Index (LAI), and Fraction of Photosynthetically Active Radiation (FPAR).
Several datasets provide global coverage of these plant productivity metrics at various spatial and temporal resolutions.
Datasets from ECOSTRESS, Global Pasture Watch, MODIS, Landsat, NOAA, OpenLandMap, PML_V2, and WAPOR are included.
Some datasets focus on specific regions like CONUS, while others provide global data.
Information about forest-dependent and tree-proximate people is also included, linked to plant and forest productivity.
