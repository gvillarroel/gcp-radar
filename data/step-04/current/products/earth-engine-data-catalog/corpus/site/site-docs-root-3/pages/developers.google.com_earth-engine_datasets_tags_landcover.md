Google Earth Engine offers a variety of land cover and land use datasets derived from satellite imagery and other sources.
These datasets cover global, regional, and specific thematic areas like agriculture, forestry, and urban environments.
Data resolutions and temporal coverages vary, ranging from high-resolution 10m data to broader 0.05-degree coverage.
The datasets are produced by numerous international organizations and initiatives, providing diverse perspectives and applications.
Examples of available datasets include global settlement maps, US forest characteristics, and regional ecosystem classifications.
