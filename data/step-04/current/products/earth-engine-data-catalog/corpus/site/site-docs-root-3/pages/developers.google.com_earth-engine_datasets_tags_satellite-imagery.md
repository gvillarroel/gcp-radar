outlined_flag
Multiple satellite imagery and related datasets are available, covering various sensors, resolutions, and processing levels.
Datasets include imagery from Landsat, ASTER, ALOS, Sentinel, MODIS, GOES, VIIRS, EMIT, EO-1, PROBA-V, Planet SkySat, and NEON.
Available data types range from raw radiance and TOA reflectance to surface reflectance, BRDF/Albedo products, and atmospheric trace gas information.
Specific datasets cater to different applications like tropical forest monitoring, cloud assessment, and emergency observation.
