outlined_flag
Multiple datasets provide information on evapotranspiration and related variables like evaporation, transpiration, humidity, and precipitation.
These datasets offer varying spatial and temporal resolutions, including daily, 3-hourly, 8-day, and monthly composites, with global or regional coverage.
Several datasets are specifically designed to assess drought conditions using derived indices.
Some datasets focus on land surface diagnostics or meteorological forcing fields to provide comprehensive climate data.
The OpenET dataset specifically offers satellite-based evapotranspiration data from multiple models and an ensemble value.
