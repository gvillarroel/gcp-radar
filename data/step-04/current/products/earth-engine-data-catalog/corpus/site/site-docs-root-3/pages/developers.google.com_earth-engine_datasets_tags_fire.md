outlined_flag
Multiple datasets are available for monitoring and analyzing fire events, including active fire detections, burned area products, and indices related to fire risk.
Several datasets provide near real-time active fire data from instruments like MODIS and VIIRS, with some offering higher spatial resolution.
Datasets are provided at various temporal resolutions, including daily, 8-day, and monthly composites, and cover global or regional extents.
Some datasets focus on historical fire regimes, intervals, and vegetation characteristics related to fire risk and succession.
Specific datasets like the Keetch-Byram Drought Index and Wildfire Risk to Communities provide information related to fire potential and the impact on communities.
