outlined_flag
The content describes various global and regional datasets related to forests, forest biomass, and carbon.
Datasets provide information on forest cover, types, age, structure, canopy height, biomass, and carbon density.
Several datasets are derived from satellite imagery like Landsat, GEDI, and PALSAR, offering different resolutions and metrics.
Data is available for various regions including global, Europe, Canada, and the contiguous United States, and often includes multiple versions or years.
The datasets support various applications such as monitoring deforestation, assessing biodiversity, conservation planning, and estimating biomass/carbon for climate reporting.
