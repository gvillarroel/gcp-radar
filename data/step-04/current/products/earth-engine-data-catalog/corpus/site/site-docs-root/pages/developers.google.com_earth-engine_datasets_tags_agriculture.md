outlined_flag
Several datasets offer insights into actual and reference evapotranspiration and interception rates.
Crop inventory and land cover data are available for Canada, the European Union, China, and the United States, including specific maps for terraces, oil palm, and irrigated lands.
Probability models estimate the likelihood of areas being occupied by specific agricultural commodities such as cocoa, coffee, palm, and rubber trees.
Datasets exist for analyzing drained organic soils and their greenhouse gas emissions related to agricultural activities.
Information on forest-proximate and tree-proximate populations, as well as global drivers of forest loss, is provided.
