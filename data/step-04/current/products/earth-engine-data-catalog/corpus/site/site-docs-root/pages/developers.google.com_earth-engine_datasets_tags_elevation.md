outlined_flag
The content provides information about various digital elevation models (DEMs) and related datasets from around the world.
Datasets vary in resolution, ranging from 0.5m to 1 arc-minute, and include both bare earth Digital Terrain Models (DTM) and Digital Surface Models (DSM) capturing features above ground.
Specific regional datasets are available for areas like the Netherlands, Australia, Canada, England, France, and the United States, often derived from high-resolution LiDAR data.
Global datasets like ASTER, ALOS, Copernicus, CryoSat-2, ETOPO1, GMTED2010, GTOPO30, MERIT DEM, NASA SRTM, NASADEM, and WWF HydroSHEDS provide diverse elevation and hydrographic information at varying resolutions.
Several derived products are listed, including those related to heat-insolation, landforms, topographic diversity, and hydrologically conditioned or void-filled DEMs.
