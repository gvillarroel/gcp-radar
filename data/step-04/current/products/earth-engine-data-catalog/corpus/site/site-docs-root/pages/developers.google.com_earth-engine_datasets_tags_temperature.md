The document provides a summary of various climate, atmospheric, and geophysical datasets available on Google Earth Engine.
Datasets cover a range of topics including reanalysis, forecasts, climate projections, land assimilation systems, and other environmental parameters.
Data sources include major organizations like ECMWF, NCEP, NASA, and NOAA, with various spatial and temporal resolutions.
The datasets include global and regional coverages, providing information on temperature, precipitation, humidity, radiation, sea surface temperature, and more.
