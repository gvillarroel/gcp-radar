This page displays the ingestion status of selected continuously-updated datasets.
A status of "OK" means data ingestion is working as expected within a margin of error.
For datasets not showing "OK", updates on the outage will be provided as issues are investigated and fixed.
This page is informational only and does not guarantee specific update or issue resolution times.
The information on this page is updated approximately every six hours.
The following table shows the ingestion pipeline status of selected
continuously-updated datasets. A status of "OK" indicates that ingestion is
working as expected within a margin of error. For non-OK datasets, updates
on the nature of the outage may be provided by our team as we investigate
and fix the issue(s). This page is informational only. While we strive to
provide the most updated data and fix any issues as soon as possible, we
cannot guarantee a specific update or issue resolution time.
