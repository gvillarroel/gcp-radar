After you add your AI agent in Producer Portal, use the
Product details tab in Producer Portal to do the following:
Add product details for your AI agent's Google Cloud Marketplace listing, such as
your AI agent's title, icon, marketing information, and links to
documentation.
Submit your AI agent's product details to Google for review. To publish your
AI agent, you must pass the Product details review.
Before you begin
To access Producer Portal, you must have completed the
Cloud Marketplace Project Info Form , provided by the Google Cloud Marketplace
team. For more information about becoming a Cloud Marketplace vendor,
see
Sign up as a Cloud Marketplace vendor .
If you can't access Producer Portal, verify that you've
selected the correct project. If you still can't access it, use the
Google Cloud Support portal to request
assistance, and include the word "Marketplace" in your description. For more
information about the Google Cloud Support portal, visit
Request assistance with Cloud Marketplace .
Add your AI agent as a product in Producer Portal. For steps,
see
Add your AI agent in Producer Portal .
Add product details
To add your AI agent's product details for its Cloud Marketplace listing,
use Producer Portal . In
Producer Portal, select your AI agent and complete the following
steps:
On the Overview page of your AI agent product, go to the
Product details section and click Edit .
Complete the Product info tab of the Product details section. While
completing Product info , you can add Category IDs. Category IDs let
customers find your product under select categories or filters as they search
Cloud Marketplace. You can add up to two Category IDs for a product.
Additionally, you must provide metadata for your AI agent, including
Industry and Business function , to improve its visibility in the
new AI-powered search experience. For more information, see
AI agent listing requirements and metadata .
If your AI agent connects to Google Distributed Cloud, add the following
text to your product info: "We recommend that you only purchase this product
for a duration equal to or less than the shortest subscription length of your
Google Distributed Cloud-connected devices."
To add tutorials, documentation, and, optionally, additional license
agreements, complete the Documentation tab of the Product details
section.
To add search metadata or keywords, or to customize the URL of the details
page for your product, complete the Product metadata tab of the
Product details section.
Cloud Marketplace might require additional approvals for some categories of
products. If you want to add your AI agent's listing to a category that isn't
listed in Producer Portal, submit a request by using the
special category request form .
Submit your product details for review
When you're ready to submit your product details for processing, click
Submit in Producer Portal .
It takes approximately two to five days for the Partner Engineering team to
review and approve your product details.
After you've submitted your product details for review, if you make additional
changes to the Product details tab, you must restart the product details
review process.
