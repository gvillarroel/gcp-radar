To publish your AI agent to Google Cloud Marketplace, you must have completed the
following steps in Producer Portal:
Complete the Product details , Pricing , and Technical integration
reviews for your AI agent.
Validate your AI agent's Agent Card.
You can submit the Product details and Pricing tabs to the
Cloud Marketplace team for review in any order. However, you must have
successfully completed the pricing review before you submit your
Technical integration tab for review.
After you've successfully completed the preceding reviews, you can publish your
AI agent to Cloud Marketplace. After you click to publish your AI agent, it
might take up to two days for your AI agent's listing to be publicly accessible
in Cloud Marketplace.
Before you begin
To access Producer Portal, you must have completed the
Cloud Marketplace Project Info Form , provided by the Google Cloud Marketplace
team. For more information about becoming a Cloud Marketplace vendor,
see
Sign up as a Cloud Marketplace vendor .
If you can't access Producer Portal, verify that you've
selected the correct project. If you still can't access it, use the
Google Cloud Support portal to request
assistance, and include the word "Marketplace" in your description. For more
information about the Google Cloud Support portal, visit
Request assistance with Cloud Marketplace .
Verify that you've successfully completed the required reviews and
validation in Producer Portal. For details, see
Overview of onboarding an AI agent .
Publish your AI agent
To submit your AI agent for review, use
Producer Portal to complete the
following steps:
In Producer Portal, go to your product's Overview page.
Click Publish .
View your AI agent's issue tracker
After your AI agent launches, your Partner Engineer creates a public issue
tracker for your product, similar to the
Google Cloud issue trackers , to track bugs and
feature requests from your users.
Make changes to your AI agent after you've submitted sections for review
If you find a mistake in your product, or if your contact at Google requests
changes to it:
Navigate to the page you'd like to make changes to.
Click Acknowledge to acknowledge that you must re-submit the
corresponding review for your product if you change it.
Make any intended changes to your product.
Re-submit the affected review for approval.
