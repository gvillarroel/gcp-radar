Eventarc lets you build event-driven architectures without having to implement,
customize, or maintain the underlying infrastructure. Eventarc is offered in two editions:
Eventarc Advanced and
Eventarc Standard .
Both editions offer a scalable eventing solution that supports a range of event providers and
destinations while managing delivery, security, authorization, observability, and error-handling
for you. For more information, see
