Jurisdictional Google Cloud console
Assured Workloads provides
control packages to help you
meet regulatory, regional, or sovereign compliance requirements. Some
control packages enforce
data residency , which keeps your data
within the jurisdiction you selected.
If your workload requires this control, always access your Google Cloud
resources using one of the jurisdictional URLs below. When you do, your
experience within the Google Cloud console may be altered when interacting with some
Google Cloud services. For example, one or more services may be hidden if they
don't fully meet your control package's data residency requirements. Other
in-page content and functionality may also be hidden or modified compared to a
workload without such requirements.
Don't enter any controlled information into free text fields, such as resource
names and search queries.
United States
console.us.cloud.google.com
For federated identity users: console.us.cloud.google
Canada
console.ca.cloud.google.com
For federated identity users: console.ca.cloud.google
European Union
console.eu.cloud.google.com
For federated identity users: console.eu.cloud.google
India
console.in.cloud.google.com
For federated identity users: console.in.cloud.google
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
console.sa.cloud.google.com
Note: Depending on the
session length
that your Google Workspace administrator has set, you may be prompted to
reauthenticate to the jurisdictional Google Cloud console through a pop-up window in
your browser. For easier reauthentication, ensure that your browser settings are
configured to allow future pop-up windows from console.sa.cloud.google.com .
For federated identity users: console.sa.cloud.google
