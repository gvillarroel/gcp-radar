Badge guidelines
Follow these guidelines whenever you're using a Google Workspace Marketplace
badge:
Use the badges as provided. Never alter the badges.
There must be clear space surrounding the badge equal to one-quarter the
height of the badge.
The badge must be large enough that all of the text is legible.
The Google Workspace Marketplace badge should be the same size or larger
than other application store badges.
Badges must be shown on a solid colored background or a background image
that doesn't obscure the badge.
Match the badge language to the language of your marketing campaign whenever
possible.
Any online use of the badge must link to the Google Workspace Marketplace.
Use the badge generator to get the HTML to include in
your digital marketing.
The badge can only be used to promote content available on
Google Workspace Marketplace.
When there's space in the creative, use a legal attribution footer stating:
"Google Workspace Marketplace and the Google Workspace Marketplace logo
are trademarks of Google LLC."
Never use the TM symbol in the legal attribution footer.
Text usage guidelines
Follow these guidelines whenever you're using the Google Workspace Marketplace
name in your marketing or communications:
When mentioning content available from the Google Workspace Marketplace,
always say "from the Google Workspace Marketplace.":
Incorrect : Our app is now available on the
Google Workspace Marketplace.
Correct : Our app is now available from
the Google Workspace Marketplace.

guidelines .
