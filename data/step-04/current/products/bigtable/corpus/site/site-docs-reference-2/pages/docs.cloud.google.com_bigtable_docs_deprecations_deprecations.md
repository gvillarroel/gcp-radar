Feature deprecations
Google Cloud's terms of service (section 7) define the deprecation
policy that applies to Bigtable. The deprecation policy only applies to
the features or services on the list of
services subject to the deprecation policy .
Once a Bigtable feature has been officially deprecated, the
feature continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the
terms of service . After this period of time, the feature or service
will be scheduled for shutdown.
The following table lists Bigtable features that were deprecated
and have been shut down.

Deprecated

v1 API
