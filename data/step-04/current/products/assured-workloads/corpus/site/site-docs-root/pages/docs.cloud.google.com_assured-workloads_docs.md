Read product documentation
Assured Workloads lets you secure your workloads and accelerate your
path to running compliant workloads on Google Cloud. Go to the Assured Workloads product page for more.
Google Cloud offers multiple administrative access controls that aim to
provide you progressively greater transparency and control over access to your
content stored in Google Cloud. The key offerings are Access Transparency,
Access Approval, and Key Access Justifications.
Access Transparency aims to provide you clear and accurate information regarding
access by Google employees to your content stored in Google Cloud. For more
information, see Access Transparency
documentation .
Access Approval aims to ensure that Google employees require your
explicit approval to access your content stored on Google Cloud. For more
information, see Access Approval
documentation
Key Access Justifications aim to give you visibility into every request for an encryption
key, a justification for that request, and a mechanism to approve or deny
decryption in the context of that request. For more information, see Overview of
Key Access Justifications .
