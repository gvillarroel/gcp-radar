Find and organize chats and files
Stay organized with collections
Save and categorize content based on your preferences.
Gemini Enterprise provides tools to search your content, star items for quick retrieval, pin agents for quick access, and manage your chat history.
Search your Gemini Enterprise content
You can use the main search bar to find your chats, generated images and videos, and other personal content within
your Gemini Enterprise workspace.
To search your content within Gemini Enterprise:
Click the search icon in the upper left to reveal the search window.
Enter your search terms.
You can search for the following:
A chat title
A phrase from a chat
A filename
A description of an image or video
An agent
Star items for quick retrieval
You can star important items, such as chats, files, or images, to help you
find them later.
To star an item:
Open a chat, file, or image in your Gemini Enterprise workspace.
Click the star_border icon.
To see all of your starred content collected in one place, click Starred .
Pin agents for quick access
To keep frequently used agents readily available, you can pin them to the top of
the list in the sidebar.
To pin an agent:
Go to Agents .
Locate the agent you want to pin, and click the
push_pin icon.
Manage your chat history
You can view and manage the recent and pinned chats that you have
had with the assistant.
To manage a chat:
In the sidebar of your Gemini Enterprise workspace, under
Chats, hover over the chat you want to manage.
Click the more_vert icon.
Choose an option:
Share: Share a previous chat with someone else in your
organization. For more information, see
Share your conversations .
Add to starred: Keep frequently used chats at the top
of your list in the sidebar.
Rename: Customize chat names to make them easier to
identify.
Delete: Remove chats from your pinned and recent lists
after they're no longer needed.
Note: Older chats are automatically deleted after the retention
period set by your administrator.
