Photorealistic 3D Maps
combine terrain (the natural landscape and elevation) with surface data
(3D structures like buildings and trees).
3D terrain data is available worldwide. 3D surface data is limited to the areas
highlighted in blue on the map below. Use the search box to check for coverage
in a specific location.
Coverage data is updated daily at 11:00 UTC.
