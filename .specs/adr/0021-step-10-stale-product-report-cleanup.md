# ADR 0021: Step 10 Stale Product Report Cleanup

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Step 10 generates final radar product reports from promoted artifacts only.
Final-output validation already treats stale `radar/products/*.md` files as
errors when they do not correspond to a promoted artifact product.

Before this decision, Step 10 wrote reports for the current promoted products
but did not remove stale product report files left from an earlier promotion
set. A regeneration could therefore leave the radar directory in a known-invalid
state until a separate cleanup occurred.

## Decision

Step 10 must make `radar/products/` match the promoted artifact product
inventory during report generation.

The canonical script, `scripts/step-10/generate-radar-reports.mjs`, removes
stale Markdown files directly under `radar/products/` when their filenames do
not match a currently promoted product slug. It then writes one product report
for each promoted product.

This cleanup is limited to direct `*.md` files in `radar/products/`. Other
radar reports are regenerated by their fixed paths, and promoted artifacts
remain the source of truth.

## Consequences

Benefits:

- Step 10 output is coherent immediately after generation.
- Final-output validation should not fail because of product reports from a
  previous promotion set.
- Targeted promotion experiments can regenerate matching radar reports without
  manual stale-file cleanup.

Costs:

- Manually maintained Markdown files directly under `radar/products/` are not
  supported; that directory is owned by Step 10.
